Where Support Goes

JLAB and StellarXplorers

Flights to nationals are covered by JLAB and StellarX program funding. Support goes toward team uniforms and meals during travel.

Raiders and Physical Skills

Ramping up for Raiders nationals in 26-27. High-wear physical equipment: static rope, military litters, carabiners, rucksacks, and hydration gear, plus competition travel support.

Drill, Color Guard, and Honor Guard

Performance rifles, specialized uniforms (ascots, gloves, berets), and transportation to meets and parades.

KHAS and MWR Service

Supplies for donation drives, food bank events, and cadet morale activities that reduce family financial burdens.

      CA-882 Today

      The Program

      CA-882 is one of the top-performing AFJROTC units in the region. After a strong 25-26, the corps is ramping programs and enrollment for 26-27, and that growth needs consistent community support.

      The unit provides citizenship training and Aerospace Science while building self-reliance, self-discipline, leadership, and character through classroom instruction, team competition, and community service. Its guiding values are Integrity First, Service Before Self, and Excellence in All We Do.

      What Your Support Builds

      25-26 set a high bar. When a community supporter joins the network for 26-27, their involvement goes beyond a single fundraiser. Here is what ongoing connection funds and enables as the corps ramps up.

      Nationals Travel

      Flights for JLAB and StellarX are covered by program funding; those teams are well-supported. The real funding gap is Raiders, which is ramping up for nationals and needs donor support to compete at the national level in 26-27.

      Equipment and Uniforms

      Raiders (including nationals prep), Drill, and Color Guard require maintained gear. Community donors make it possible for every cadet to compete and serve at full readiness.

      Recognition and Connection

      We're shaping how supporters are thanked, from notes of appreciation to public acknowledgments, so passive contact can grow into real investment in the corps.

      Local Business Sponsorship

      Local businesses can invest in CA-882 while we finalize recognition options with leadership. Reach a tight-knit community and support cadets at the same time.
